Privacy policy

rngwillbake POS · Last updated November 2026

rngwillbake POS is the internal point-of-sale app used by the rngwillbake bake shop on its own tablet. It is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

The app keeps the shop's menu, stock, orders, customer names and notes typed by staff, payment reference numbers, payment proof photos, and settings. All of this is saved on the tablet itself. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server.

Google Drive backup

If the shop connects Google Drive, the app uses Google's “drive.file” permission to save backup copies of the shop's data into a folder it creates in the shop's own Google Drive. With this permission the app can only see and change files it created; it cannot read any other files in that Google Drive. The shop can disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app's Settings, or remove access from its Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reading payment photos

When staff photograph a payment confirmation, the reference number is read on the tablet itself. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Order slips are only printed or shared when staff choose to.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact rngwillbake on Instagram at @rngwillbake.
